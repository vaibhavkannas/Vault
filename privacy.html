Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Vault is a personal document storage app. This page explains what data it accesses, where it's stored, and how you're in control of it.

What Vault accesses, and why

  • Your basic Google profile (name, email address, profile photo) — used only to identify you within the app and show your account in the interface.
  • Files and folders Vault itself creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file scope) — Vault can only see and manage the "Vault" folder and files it creates there. It cannot see, list, or modify any other file in your Drive.
  • Your overall Drive storage quota (the drive.metadata.readonly scope, read-only) — used only to show the storage-usage bar in Settings. This does not let Vault see the names or contents of your other files.

Vault never requests access to your full Google Drive, Gmail, contacts, or any other Google data beyond what's listed above.

Where your data lives

  • Your actual documents (photos, scans, PDFs) are stored as ordinary files in your own Google Drive, under the "Vault" folder Vault creates there. They are never copied to any other server.
  • Document details (titles, categories, expiry dates, and similar metadata — not the files themselves) are stored in Google Cloud Firestore, under an account record tied to your unique Google user ID.
  • Persistent Drive access: so you don't have to re-approve access every time you open Vault, a Drive refresh token is stored server-side in a Google Apps Script project, associated with your account ID. This runs entirely on Google's own infrastructure and is never shared with any other party.
  • Text recognition (OCR), used to help fill in details automatically when you add a document, runs entirely inside your browser using open-source software. Your document images are never uploaded to any OCR or AI service.

What Vault does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No human ever reviews your documents.

Sharing a document

Vault can hand a document's actual file to any app on your device via your device's own share sheet, or download it directly. Sharing a document never grants anyone broader access to the rest of your Vault.

Your controls

  • Sign out any time from the Settings screen.
  • Revoke Drive access any time from your Google Account's third-party access page — this immediately invalidates Vault's stored access.
  • Delete everything — Settings includes a permanent delete option that removes all of your document records, your Vault folder in Drive, and your account, in one step.

Children's privacy

Vault is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change too. Continued use of Vault after an update means you accept the revised policy.
